Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how KICKERS (“we,” “us”) handles information when you visit kickers.lol and use the KICKERS browser game and online features. Please read it together with our Terms of Service.

1. Information we handle

  • Sign-in information: If you choose to sign in with X, our authentication provider processes account and identity information needed to authenticate you. We may receive a display name or X username and an authentication status. We do not request or store your X password.
  • Gameplay and multiplayer data: During an online session, the game may transmit your player identifier, display name, character appearance, and real-time game state (such as position and rotation) to other participants and our multiplayer service so the match can function.
  • Information stored on your device: The game uses browser local storage for preferences such as sound and volume, your locally saved character customization, a generated player identifier, and locally stored game progress or statistics where supported.
  • Technical information: The hosting, security, and network services that deliver the website may process standard connection data such as IP address, browser/device details, timestamps, and request logs.

2. How information is used

We use this information to provide sign-in, operate matches and multiplayer rooms, remember local preferences and character settings, maintain game functionality, protect the Service against abuse, troubleshoot errors, and respond to support requests.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that support authentication, hosting, security, and multiplayer connectivity. In online matches, information needed to identify and display your player and character is visible to other participants. We may disclose information if required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect users, the Service, or our legal rights.

4. Third-party services

Sign-in is provided through third-party authentication technology and X. Those providers may collect and process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Your use of their services is also subject to those policies. Please review the relevant provider policies before connecting an account.

5. Cookies and local storage

KICKERS currently uses browser local storage for game settings, a player identifier, character customization, and locally maintained game data. Authentication or hosting providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver and secure their services. Your browser settings can help you manage or clear locally stored information, although doing so may reset preferences or local progress and may affect sign-in.

6. Retention and security

Local settings remain on your device until you clear them or browser storage is removed. Multiplayer session information is used while the relevant session is active; technical logs may be retained by service providers according to their own policies. We use reasonable measures appropriate to a small online game, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to sign in, disconnect from a match, or clear KICKERS site data in your browser. You may contact us to ask about access, correction, or deletion requests. We will assess and respond as required by applicable law. Some information may remain with third-party providers or in technical backups subject to their retention policies.

8. Children's privacy

KICKERS is not intended for children under 13 or the higher minimum age required by local law. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children below the applicable minimum age. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

9. International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards should be applied by the relevant provider.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as the Service changes. The “Last updated” date indicates the latest revision. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect is subject to the updated Policy, as permitted by law.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact KICKERS through @kickerszone on X.
