Community Guidelines

KICKERS is built for competitive, friendly fun. These guidelines apply to player names, match behavior, chat, and any content shared through KICKERS.

Play fair

  • Do not use cheats, exploits, scripts, bots, modified clients, or unauthorized tools to gain an unfair advantage.
  • Do not manipulate scores, rankings, match outcomes, or player identities.
  • Do not disrupt matches, overload servers, or interfere with another player's connection.

Respect other players

  • No harassment, threats, bullying, hate speech, or targeted abuse.
  • Do not impersonate another player, public figure, or KICKERS staff member.
  • Keep names and messages appropriate for a shared gaming environment.
  • Do not share another person's private information or encourage others to do so.

Keep content safe

  • Do not post unlawful, sexually exploitative, violent-threat, hateful, or rights-infringing content.
  • Do not use names or messages to advertise scams, phishing links, malware, or spam.
  • Only use images and content you have the right to use.

Enforcement

We may investigate reported behavior and take proportionate action, including removing content, limiting access to features, or suspending access to KICKERS. Serious or repeated violations may result in longer restrictions. We may also take action to protect the stability and safety of the Service.

Report a concern

To report abuse, cheating, or a safety concern, contact KICKERS through @kickerszone on X. Include the player name, approximate time, and a brief description. Avoid sending sensitive personal information.
